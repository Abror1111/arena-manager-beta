Original CS2 combat particle materials adapted for phones and PC.

216 genuine Molotov flame/ground-fire, C4 flame/smoke, HE dust, flare and scorch frames are packed into one bounded atlas. Source alpha and additive-layer handling retained; flash uses an exported flare substitute because its referenced aircraft_blue texture was absent. The Source2 particle engine and motion-vector pipeline are not included.

Phone/PC VFX and observer regression, offline/project-prefix web behavior, all embedded assets, signature, alignment and upgrade signer are checked before deployment. Same Android package and club-save format; install over1.13 to retain your club. All maps included for offline play. Ancient Night remains simplified3D. Physical Android GPU performance is not measured in the cloud.

Original CS2 assets belong to Valve and respective owners. Source hashes and adaptations: materials/vfx/combat-provenance.json.
