# ARENA Manager 1.24 beta

The newer 180-file user CS2 audio export is integrated, with SHA256 verification and provenance per recording. There are 75 original effect/reload/ambient cues and four original Valve music cues. New C4/HE, molotov, flash, smoke, planting, P2000, Dual Berettas, Desert Eagle and M4A1-S recordings replace earlier authored fills. Nine more reload cues are wired. Menu and MVP music now use supplied original recordings; the menu is a documented 119.5-second excerpt. [Audio details and remaining authored cues](AUDIO-AUTOMATIC.md).

Original explosion cues take priority under heavy gunfire. Cached decoded effects stay at 32kHz and within 24MiB even on higher-rate output devices. PCM compatibility recordings support devices unable to decode Ogg. Settings includes bomb/grenade audition choices. Old personal imports that mistook radio voices or object impacts for explosions cannot override correctly bundled effects.

The 1.23 terrain and camera fixes remain: complete render ground under all eleven layouts, exact source floor backing 0.09 units lower, source-empty sparse surroundings, source paving/roof materials and a supporting volume under Vertigo. Route-height cutaways retain full original exterior roofs while keeping playable corridors visible. The ten source GLBs, textures, source navigation, map terrain/masks and agent assets are unchanged. Ancient Night remains simplified. Terrain/scenery additions are ARENA reconstructions, not a claim of complete 1:1 CS2 map geometry.

Install over the previous app to retain the club. Package ID, upgrade signing identity and career schema remain. Browser and native package checks are recorded; physical Android performance/output requires a device test. The separate working case bot is untouched. Original game assets belong to Valve/respective owners; authored audio and added geometry have separate provenance.

Install over the existing ARENA app to keep your club. Do not uninstall first.
