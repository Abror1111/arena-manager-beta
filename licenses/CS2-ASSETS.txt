The CS2 radar images, navigation data, collision geometry and overview transforms
are Valve Corporation game assets, extracted and published by awpy-data.
They are not covered by the MIT licenses for the extraction/query scripts.
This noncommercial prototype is not affiliated with or endorsed by Valve.
No Esports Manager 2026 assets or player portraits are included.

Source: https://github.com/pnxenopoulos/awpy-data/releases/tag/2000933
CS2 ClientVersion 2000933, patch1.41.9.1, Steam build25966348.
Archive SHA256 values and per-map derived mesh checksums: /cs2/provenance.json.
Dust II additionally contains the user-exported original Source2Viewer20.1 visual
scene derived into a mobile GLB, with original albedo textures. Other ten maps
retain the decimated collision export. Original asset rights remain with Valve
or the respective map owner; these assets are not MIT licensed.
A/B and spawn annotations were inspected on the original radars, then resolved
onto valid reachable navigation polygons; they are not extracted entity data.
The nav-derived eye-height visibility table is sampled at area centroids and
is an approximation for moving players. Source mesh/nav files remain separate
from saved career snapshots. Dust II mobile geometry is simplified, instance tints are preserved in vertex
colors, albedo textures resized/WebP encoded, and normal/ORM maps omitted.
Original engine effect cards and compiler brushes are excluded. Source2 shaders
and lighting are approximated in glTF. Other maps use procedural materials and
blended source radar. This is not a claim of exact Source2 renderer parity.

CT SAS and T Phoenix additionally use user-exported original CS2 agents, skin
weights, skeletons and66 selected world animation clips per agent. Original
third-person vertices are retained; first-person arms and empty animation helper
meshes are excluded. Original albedo is resized/WebP95 encoded; normal/ORM maps
are omitted. Source and mobile checksums are in /agents/provenance.json. These
models and animations are Valve game assets, not MIT-licensed ARENA artwork.

ARENA1.11: genuine scenes for Mirage, Vertigo, Nuke, Anubis, Ancient, Inferno, Overpass, Cache and Train were exported from the user's installed CS2 client2000933 with Source2Viewer-CLI20.1. Original map-owner/Valve ownership applies. Source archives are preserved separately; mobile geometry and original albedo textures are derived, not Source2 shader replicas. Dust II retains its earlier verified mobile scene. See visuals/provenance.json. Ancient Night still uses its separate source navigation and simplified collision presentation.

ARENA1.12: selected source-map crate, barrel, chair, basket and pottery triangles retain their original geometry, UVs and albedo. A bounded Arena simulation adds impulses, fracture grouping and charring to lightweight decorations; structural cover/navigation are retained. No source map/model files were modified. The 36 weapon silhouettes and Arena combat/particle code are original procedural approximations; actual CS2 weapon models, weapon sounds and Source2 physics are not included.

ARENA 1.13 VFX: the Ancient candle flame atlas is an unmodified user-exported CS2 image. See materials/vfx/provenance.json for source archive/image hashes and material animation settings. This is a candle texture, not the original Molotov particle definition. Grenade, smoke, flash and fire simulation/rendering are ARENA approximations; the Source2 particle engine is not included.
