# ARENA1.21 beta — original audio playback and continuous map edges

32 verified original CS2 effects now preload while the map is loading. MAG-7 was incorrectly excluded by a filename filter and is restored. Previously a failed initial load was cached permanently and silently fell back to synthesis. Failed cues now retry, report their readiness and can be retried manually. Bundled cues never become synthetic gunshots while loading. Gunshots can replace low-priority ambient voices or old tails within the existing14/22voice cap. A less aggressive output limiter and stronger original-recording level retain the shot transient.

Each effect has a content-addressed PCM WAV compatibility copy derived from its verified original recording. If a browser cannot decode Ogg, it plays that genuine recording as PCM. Android serves audio MIME types explicitly and allows media playback inside the trusted packaged game while preserving in-game mute/volume. Settings includes «CS2 ovozini eshitish» to audition original AK/AWP/Glock recordings. Music remains the two genuine C4 cues. The supplied export still lacks seven weapon shots, complete reloads, original grenade/C4 explosion cues and menu/result tracks; unavailable effects keep labelled procedural fallback and unavailable music stays silent. No other weapon's recording is falsely relabelled as a missing original.

Four raised rectangular exterior bands, their two-unit gaps and straight border rails are replaced by one continuous ground apron following the convex playable perimeter. Its heights meet the map rim and gradually merge into distant ground. Both use the same world-space texture coordinates. Scenery is grounded on that surface. Only non-playable exterior space changes; no playable floor, original source mesh or navigation is filled or modified. Vertigo retains its city drop. This is a visual continuation, not newly playable terrain or a reconstruction of absent source buildings.

Existing double-sided opaque walls, closed-contour lower-floor caps, soft color lift, transparent water/wakes/spray, detailed recreated C4/utility models,43 pinned CS2 vector icons, original exported maps/agents and career remain. Source GLB/texture/navigation bytes, save schema2 and Android upgrade signer are preserved. Ancient Night remains simplified.

Validation includes genuine decoded audio at the output analyser, transient download recovery, a simulated unsupported Ogg decoder with all32 genuine PCM fallbacks, source integrity, all11static map views, exact embedded APK assets/signature and browser prefix/offline behavior. Android-like WebView/codec testing in Chromium does not measure physical Android speakers or performance. Install over the existing app to retain your club.
