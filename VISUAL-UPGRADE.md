# ARENA1.19 — C4, utilities, icons, water and clearer movement

C4 is now a detailed original geometry recreation with four wrapped explosive blocks, straps, colored cables, keypad, LCD and a separate blinking LED. Its screen shows arming, the remaining planted timer, or SAFE after defuse. The same design appears in the planting agent's hand and on the ground. HE, flash, smoke, Molotov and CT incendiary have separate recognizable models, levers/pins and bands. A short hand-to-flight presentation, visible flight trails and the existing physical wall/floor ricochet make utility use readable. Plant/defuse HUD progress uses equipment icons. These3D equipment meshes are our recreations, not ripped CS2 model binaries.

The UI uses43 genuine local CS2 equipment SVGs, including all36 weapon silhouettes and7 utility/kit icons. Source: Juknum/counter-strike-icons, pinned commitb198fd2cbe58274c0826fdf299e1c17783335bde. Each source URL, byte count and SHA256 is retained in icons/cs2/provenance.json; upstream LICENSE.txt is included. Icon assets belong to Valve and are community/informational assets, not licensed under the tooling MIT license. No icon assets are fetched from external servers during play.

Water overlays use the exact original csgo_water_fancy.vfx surface triangles and node/world transforms from the user's preserved map exports. Anubis has981 triangles, Ancient473, Inferno34, Nuke29 and Train142. The runtime shader recreates gentle moving highlights and bounded footstep/explosion rings; it is not Source2's native reflection/refraction engine. We do not invent water in dry maps or paint it over rectangular ground regions. Bridge-level height checks avoid splashes above water. Source GLB, texture, collision and navigation bytes remain unchanged. See visuals/water-provenance.json.

Daylight has modestly higher exposure, fill and directional light. Existing oblique static, automatic and individual-player cameras remain. Agent bodies and labels interpolate only between the last two authoritative physics positions, with wall/height and new-round guards; simulation, save data and tactics are unchanged. Facing arrows and the selected live player's actual queued route clarify movement. Full map water, source-agent animation, grenade/C4 and route displays are checked at mobile and PC sizes; physical Android performance is not measured by browser emulation.

All118 features remain, including31 original CS2 effects and2 original C4 music cues, separate volume settings, club manager/economy/experience, source agents and map viewer. Menu/result tracks and original explosion/full-reload recordings were absent from the audio export; missing effects still use procedural fallback. Ancient Night still has simplified3D.

The production build now separates unchanged, losslessly compressed navigation into a stable shared chunk so future UI updates can reuse it. The existing source VFX atlas and equipment icons are also included in verified shared downloads for offline use. Original exports, historical published asset URLs, career schema2/key and Android upgrade signer are preserved. Install over the previous app to keep the club.
