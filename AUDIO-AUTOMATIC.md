# ARENA 1.24 audio

The newer user export `ARENA-CS2-AUDIO-20261022-234140.zip` is now integrated. All 180 exported file sizes, SHA256 hashes and ZIP CRCs were checked. Archive SHA256: `5a202f9cf90d100b726740956c9feec8d1f121753b5433f9d5ec27aa2f2e46a7`. Earlier unrelated recordings are retained with their own archive provenance.

The runtime contains 75 original CS2 effect/reload/ambient cues and four original Valve music cues. New original recordings replace authored C4/HE detonations, flash ringing, smoke detonation, molotov detonation, planting, and P2000, Dual Berettas, Desert Eagle and M4A1-S shots. Nine additional reload cues cover AK, M4 variants, P2000, Dual Berettas, MP7, MP9, P90 and Galil. Each is mapped to a real exported action recording; these are not claims of complete reload sequences. The available smoke recording is a distant detonation.

Valve menu music, MVP anthem and both C4 music cues play from verified streamed MP3 bytes. The menu is an explicitly recorded 119.5-second excerpt with a one-second fade, fitting the existing music budget. No music is decoded into long PCM buffers. MP5-SD, Zeus, knife, some supplementary effects, battle background and loss score remain separately labelled ARENA creations. Original source keys always take precedence; no authored recording is labelled CS2.

Effects are decoded at 32kHz, independent of the output device's sample rate, and remain within a 24MiB decoded-buffer budget (approximately 16.2MiB in browser measurements). Every new effect has a verified PCM compatibility copy for unsupported Ogg devices. C4/HE get priority over saturated gunfire voices. Legacy local radio-line/physics-impact misclassifications cannot replace the correctly bundled explosion and beep cues. The source ZIP and its unused files are not added to the game bundle.

Settings can audition weapons and individual bomb/grenade cues, with their source label. Tests trace actual decoded-buffer hashes, measure WebAudio output, exercise codec failure and recovery, verify battle-event routing under gunfire saturation, play original menu/MVP/C4 music and reopen cached maps/audio offline under the GitHub project prefix. Browser emulation does not measure a physical Android speaker or establish subjective 1:1 similarity.
