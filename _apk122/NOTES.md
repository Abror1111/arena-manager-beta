# ARENA Manager 1.22 beta

Original CS2 audio coverage expands from 32 to 56 effects/reload/ambient recordings, with the original two C4 tracks retained. The AK uses the newer indexed recording. Reload and per-map ambience recordings now play in actual combat. A separately attributed ARENA sound library fills missing effects and adds menu, match and result music. Shot-priority mixing, combat music ducking, proper MP3 MIME handling and selectable weapon auditions improve clarity. ARENA sound design is never counted or labelled as genuine CS2 audio.

All eleven map settings now have detailed instanced scenery kits: sandstone/plaster quarters, terracotta houses, factories and tanks, railway wagons, jungle ruins, river temples, park apartments and the Vertigo skyline. Buildings have windows, parapets, rooftop equipment, doorsteps and roof details. Original source wall/roof/stone textures are reused for compatible scenery materials after map load. A continuous ground apron retains the existing perimeter integration.

Safe corner pockets inside the rectangular view are filled only outside the convex navigation footprint, with a full clearance margin. Twenty additional roof details across six maps are derived from flat original collision surfaces and excluded from all navigation polygons. These are decorative additions, not new routes or collision blockers. The authentic ten mobile GLBs, source texture bytes, water surfaces, agents, navigation and career schema remain unchanged. Ancient Night still uses the simplified map representation. This update does not claim every internal void or map boundary is an exact CS2 reconstruction.

The current save key/schema and APK application ID/signing identity are retained. Install over the existing app; do not uninstall first. The working case bot is outside this project and is not changed.

Testing covers all eleven source-map views on landscape phone dimensions, draw-call/triangle budgets, nav clearance and deterministic placement, sound provenance/output, offline reopening and package upgrade integrity. Physical Android performance and subjective audio similarity require a device test. Source models, recordings and textures belong to Valve/respective owners; new scenery kits and compositions are ARENA recreations.

Install over the existing ARENA app to keep your club. Do not uninstall first.
